Questions buyers ask us first.

Don’t see yours? Send it with the form below and a loan officer will answer the same business day.

How much do I need for a down payment?

It depends on the program. VA and USDA loans can be zero down, FHA starts at 3.5%, and conventional loans start at 3% for qualified first-time buyers. Plan for closing costs on top, usually 2–5% of the price.

Does a pre-approval hurt my credit?

A full pre-approval includes a credit check, which can cause a small, temporary dip. Mortgage checks within a short shopping window are generally counted as one inquiry.

How does Florida home insurance affect my loan?

Insurance is part of your monthly payment and your qualifying ratio, so we price it early. Homes with newer roofs, impact windows and a wind-mitigation report usually cost less to insure.

Can I buy a condo with a mortgage?

Yes. Lenders review the building’s budget, reserves and inspection reports. We check those before you spend money on an appraisal, so there are no late surprises.

How long does it take to close?

Most purchases close in about 30 days from contract. A fully documented pre-approval can make that faster.
